Let \(f:[0,w]\to\mathbb C\) be \(C^2\), let \(w=mh\), and let \(m\) be a positive integer. Then
\[
\left|
\sum_{j=1}^m f(jh)
-\frac1h\int_0^wf(v)\,dv
-\frac{f(w)-f(0)}2
\right|
\le
\frac h8\int_0^w|f''(v)|\,dv.
\tag{SOL.5.1}
\]

### Proof

On one interval \([a,a+h]\),
\[
\frac h2(f(a)+f(a+h))-\int_a^{a+h}f(v)\,dv
=
\frac12\int_a^{a+h}(v-a)(a+h-v)f''(v)\,dv.
\]
Since
\[
0\le\frac12(v-a)(a+h-v)\le\frac{h^2}{8},
\]
summing over the \(m\) cells gives
\[
\left|T_h-\int_0^wf\right|
\le
\frac{h^2}{8}\int_0^w|f''|.
\]
Finally,
\[
\frac{T_h}{h}
=
\sum_{j=1}^m f(jh)-\frac{f(w)-f(0)}2.
\]
Divide by \(h\). ∎

The constant \(1/8\) cannot in general be replaced by \(1/12\) for an \(L^1\)-bound.
