## SOL.1. Exact modulus formula for the tilted Mahonian characteristic function

Let \(Y_j\) have tilted law
\[
\Pr_\lambda(Y_j=\ell)

 \frac{e^{-\lambda\ell}}
 {\sum_{r=0}^{j-1}e^{-\lambda r}},

\]
and let \(\phi_\lambda(t)\) be the characteristic function of the centered sum \(\sum_{j=1}^mY_j\). Centering changes only the phase.

For \(\lambda>0\),
\[
\boxed{
|\phi_\lambda(t)|
=
\prod_{j=1}^{m}
\left(
\frac{1+\dfrac{\sin^2(jt/2)}{\sinh^2(j\lambda/2)}}

\tag{1}
\]

### Proof

For \(z=\lambda-it\),
\[
\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}e^{-z\ell}

Moreover,
\[
|\sinh(x-iy)|^2=\sinh^2x+\sin^2y.
\]
Dividing the modulus at \(z=\lambda-it\) by that at \(z=\lambda\), and multiplying over \(j\), gives (1). ∎
