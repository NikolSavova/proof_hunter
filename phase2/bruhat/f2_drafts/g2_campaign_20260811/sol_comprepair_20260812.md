# SOL.4. Correct bootstrap lemma

**Lemma SOL.4 (convex two-endpoint bootstrap).** Let \(0<a<b\), let \(x\in[0,b]\), and let \(G:[a,b]\to\mathbb R\) be convex. Assume

\[
x\le G(x)\quad\text{whenever }x\in[a,b],
\]

and

\[
G(a)<a,\qquad G(b)<b.
\]

Then \(x\le a\).

### Proof

Suppose \(x>a\). Then \(x\in(a,b]\). Write

\[
x=(1-\theta)a+\theta b,\qquad 0<\theta\le1.
\]

By convexity,

\[
G(x)
\le
(1-\theta)G(a)+\theta G(b)
<
(1-\theta)a+\theta b
=x.
\]

This contradicts \(x\le G(x)\). Therefore \(x\le a\). ∎

### Application

Under (S4), \(x\le b=89/100\). Under (S6), Lemma SOL.4 applies with

\[
a=\frac{20}{m},
\qquad
b=\frac{89}{100},
\qquad
G=\mathcal G_\omega.
\]

It follows that

\[
x\le\frac{20}{m}.
\]

Convexity together with \(G(20/m)<20/m\) alone would not suffice. The second endpoint inequality

\[
G(89/100)<89/100
\]

is essential. Monotonicity of \(G\) is not needed for the chord argument.
