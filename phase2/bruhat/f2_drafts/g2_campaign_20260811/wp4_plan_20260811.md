## 0. Standing notation, frame, and the split geometry (used by all sub-lemmas)

- `U_j^lam` (`j = 1..m`): independent, `P(U_j = i) = e^{-lam i}/z_j` on
  `{0,...,j-1}`, `z_j = (1-e^{-lam j})/(1-e^{-lam})`; `S_lam = sum_j U_j^lam`;
  `mu(lam) = E S_lam`, `s2 = sigma_lam^2 = Var S_lam`; `kappa_r = kappa_r(lam)`
  the r-th cumulant of `S_lam`; `q := e^{-lam}`; `w := m lam`;
  `A := lam^2 s2` (the square of the "radius-to-width ratio"; the whole plan's
  error currency is `u := 1/A`).
- Tilt frame: `lam(k)` is the unique solution of `mu(lam) = k` (Lemma 3.1,
  merged draft — cited-PROVED). By the mirror symmetry `r(N-k) = r(k)`,
  `lam(N-k) = -lam(k)`, WLOG `lam > 0` everywhere below. Tilt invariance:
  `r(k) = p(k)^2/(p(k-1) p(k+1))` with `p(x) := P(S_lam = x)` at `lam = lam(k)`
  (mean-matched: `mu = k` exactly).
- Centered cf: `phi(t) := E e^{it(S_lam - k)}`; per-factor
  `nu_j(t) = z_j^{-1} (1 - e^{(it-lam)j})/(1 - e^{it-lam})` (T2 Lemma T.6(i)
  convention). Fourier inversion: `p(k + d) = (1/2pi) int_{-pi}^{pi}
  phi(t) e^{-i t d} dt`, `d in {-1, 0, +1}`.
- **The residual band** `B(m) := { lam : 4/m < lam <= 0.89 }` (equivalently
  `w > 4`), `m >= 401`. This is exactly the scope P.8 + Theorem S's R2 row
  leave to wp4 (`wp3_draft_a2.md` §5–§6.1).
- **`w`-bands** (fixed for the whole package): `W1 = (4,5]`, `W2 = (5,6]`,
  `W3 = (6,8]`, `W4 = (8,10]`, `W5 = (10,20]`, `W6b = (20,40]`,
  `W7 = (40, infty)` (each intersected with `lam <= 0.89`).
- **Split geometry of `[0, pi]`** (fixed): core `[0, lam/2]` (SL1 model);
  mid `[lam/2, 0.8 lam]` (SL3(i) Gaussian domination); crossover
  `[0.8 lam, t_0(lam)]` (wp1-c Clause W.6), `t_0(lam) = 2 arcsin(sinh(lam/2))`;
  far `[t_0(lam), pi]` (wp1-c Corollary W.5(ii), floor `exp(-0.0373 m)`).
  Note `t_0(lam) <= 1.08 lam` on `lam <= 0.89` and `M = m sin(t/2) >= 0.389 w
  > 1.55` at `t = 0.8 lam` for `w > 4` — W.6's `M > 1` hypothesis holds on the

## SL4 — Kernel/inversion assembly: the ratio law with an explicit ledger

**Statement (to prove).** Let `m >= 401`, `lam = lam(k) in B(m)` for interior
`k` (mean-matched frame, §0). Assume SL1, SL2, SL3 as stated. Then

```
s2 (r(k) - 1) = 1 + theta * L(w-band, A, m) * u ,   |theta| <= 1 ,  u = 1/A ,

     + 0.2 + 1.0 + 0.36 sqrt(A) A e^{-0.0373 m}
     [k4]    [k3^2]      [R5]          [mid tail]
     [W.6 tail] [assembly slop S_alg] [far]
```

where the `S_alg = 1.0` line is SL4's own property: it must contain ALL of
(a) the exponential-to-polynomial model expansion error (expanding
`e^{-i kappa_3 t^3/6 + kappa_4 t^4/24}` to second order in the phase under the
Gaussian weight; the cubes contribute `<= 1.1 (R31*)^3/sqrt(A)`),
(b) the second-order/denominator terms of the ratio algebra
`r - 1 = D/(p_- p_+)` (products of two first-order buckets, each `O(u)`),
(c) the `[-pi,pi] -> R` extension tails of the model integrals (Gaussian
`Q(sqrt(A)/2)`-class).
The prover states and certifies the split of `S_alg = 1.0` among (a)-(c).
(The pure-Gaussian model's own `+1/(2s2)`-class term — `A/(2s2) <= lam^2/2
<= 0.4` in `u`-units — is NOT slop: it sits inside the COMPUTED main term
`eta` of step (3) below, which must be calculated, not bounded, to relative
`O(u^2)`.)

**Suggested route (the g1 B.5–B.7 pattern, tilted).** Work with
`D(k) := p(k)^2 - p(k-1)p(k+1)`, `r - 1 = D/(p_-p_+)`. Model
`G(t) := e^{-s2 t^2/2} (1 - i kappa_3 t^3/6 + kappa_4 t^4/24 -
kappa_3^2 t^6/72)` (polynomial-times-Gaussian — every model integral is a
closed-form Hermite moment; no exponential-model integrability issues). Define
`qhat(d) := (1/2pi) int_R G(t) e^{-itd} dt`. Steps: (1) `p(k+d) = qhat(d) +
(box error on [0, lam/2] from SL1's R5 + phase-cube expansion) + (tail error
from SL3(ii)) + (R-extension error)`; (2) `Dhat := qhat(0)^2 -
qhat(-1)qhat(+1)` in closed form; the `kappa_3`-linear terms cancel in `Dhat`
and in `p_-p_+` by the `d -> -d` symmetry (the mean-matched analogue of the
odd-cumulant cancellation — this is why the ledger carries `(R31*)^2`, not
`R31*`); (3) `s2 Dhat/(qhat_- qhat_+) = 1 + eta` with `eta` EXPLICIT
(`= (e^{1/s2}-1)s2 - 1`-type Gaussian factor plus `kappa_3, kappa_4`
corrections, all computed, total `|eta| <= [R42*/2 + 0.3(R31*)^2 +
1/(2s2) ] u`); (4) transfer: the kernel identity of g1-B.5 applied to the
even part plus direct bounding of the odd part — or, simpler and endorsed:
bound `|D - Dhat|` and `|p(k+d) - qhat(d)|` directly by the SL1/SL3 buckets
(each error inherits the RELATIVE size because `qhat(0) >= (1 -
0.3u)/sqrt(2pi s2)` — kept two-sided). The `0.3 (R31*)^2`-coefficient: the
`kappa_3^2 t^6/72` Hermite moment `15/72 = 0.208` plus the cube-cross terms
rounded up. Keep every inequality two-sided so the full `|theta| <= 1` form
lands (the lower-bound-only fallback is automatic).
