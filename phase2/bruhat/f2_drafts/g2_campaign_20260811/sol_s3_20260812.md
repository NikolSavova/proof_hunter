## SOL.1. Exact scaled-cumulant representation

For \(n=2,3,4\), define

\[
\phi_n(x)=\sum_{k\ge1}k^{\,n-1}e^{-kx},
\qquad
h_n(x)=x^n\phi_n(x).
\]

Explicitly, with \(q=e^{-x}\),

\[
h_2(x)=x^2\frac{q}{(1-q)^2},
\]

\[
h_3(x)=x^3\frac{q(1+q)}{(1-q)^3},
\]

\[
h_4(x)=x^4\frac{q(1+4q+q^2)}{(1-q)^4}.
\]

The removable values at \(x=0\) are

\[
h_2(0)=1,\qquad h_3(0)=2,\qquad h_4(0)=6.
\]

### Lemma SOL.1

For the tilted Mahonian law,

\[
\lambda^n\kappa_n
=
m h_n(\lambda)-\sum_{j=1}^{m}h_n(j\lambda),
\qquad n=2,3,4.
\]

Consequently, if

\[
F_n(w,\lambda)
:=
\lambda\left(
m h_n(\lambda)-\sum_{j=1}^{m}h_n(j\lambda)
\right),
\qquad w=m\lambda,
\]

then

\[
r_{31}=\frac{|F_3|}{F_2},
\qquad
r_{42}=\frac{F_4}{F_2},
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{
J(w,\lambda)
=
\frac{F_3(w,\lambda)^2}{F_2(w,\lambda)^2}
-\frac{F_4(w,\lambda)}{2F_2(w,\lambda)}.
}
\tag{SOL.1}
\]

#### Proof

For the \(j\)-th factor,

\[
Z_j(\lambda)=\frac{1-e^{-j\lambda}}{1-e^{-\lambda}}.
\]

Differentiating \(\log Z_j\), with the standard alternating signs induced by the parameter \(e^{-\lambda S}\), gives

\[
\kappa_n^{(j)}
=
\phi_n(\lambda)-j^n\phi_n(j\lambda),
\qquad n\ge2.
\]

Summing over \(j\) and multiplying by \(\lambda^n\) proves the first identity. The ratios follow because the common extra factor \(\lambda\) cancels between \(F_2,F_3,F_4\). ∎
