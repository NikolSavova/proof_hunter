## Repair 1 — ✅ CLOSED (referee issue 1: small-m pointwise coverage)
**Obligation:** Cor B.4's pointwise bound with C₁ = 0.45 was proved for m ≥ 110 but only
*asserted* for 4 ≤ m ≤ 109.
**Done:** exact verification for EVERY m in 4..109 and EVERY k:
σ·m²·|E(k)| ≤ 0.45 where E(k) = p(k) − (2πλ)^{-1/2} e^{-y²/2}[1 − b·He₄(y)],

Mahonian rows by exact integer DP; analytics in `decimal`, run at BOTH 50 and 100 digits
(agreement 1.78e−46 vs margins ~0.1 — no precision risk; no catastrophic cancellation since
log/div inputs are exact big-integer ratios).
**Result: ALL PASS (106/106 m).** Global max = 0.10291 at m=4, k=4 (hand-verified) — margin
factor > 4.3 everywhere; decreasing to ~0.0675 by m=109.
**Script:** `g1b_scripts/repair1_pointwise_m4to109_20260802.py` (~5 s, stdlib, runs anywhere).
