# Erdős 838 prior-art kill-search — 2026-08-12

## Verdict

No public source found in this search states either the Pascal-row coefficient

\[
\frac{1}{2\ln 2}=0.7213475204\ldots
\]

or the stronger central-cell coefficient

\[
1-\frac{1}{4\ln 2}=0.6393262398\ldots .
\]

The central-cell bound is therefore a high-confidence *apparently novel*
quantitative improvement, not yet a claim of exhaustive global novelty. A
MathSciNet citation search and confirmation from an expert in the
Erdős--Szekeres literature remain appropriate before submission.

## Baseline located

With base-2 logarithms, the strongest explicit public window located was

\[
\frac14\leq\liminf
\frac{\log_2 f(N)}{(\log_2N)^2}
\leq\limsup
\frac{\log_2 f(N)}{(\log_2N)^2}
\leq1.
\]

The lower constant follows by combining the modern
`ES(k)=2^{k+o(k)}` theorem with the standard double count. The upper constant
comes from the classical `2^{k-2}`-point construction and counting all subsets
of size at most `log_2 N+O(1)`. The 1978 argument already implies constants
`1/8` and `1`; the `1/4` lower constant is recorded in the April 2026 public
discussion.

## Log-base warning

If the normalization instead uses natural logarithms, a base-2 coefficient
`a` becomes `a/ln 2`. Thus the new coefficient is

\[
\frac{1-1/(4\ln2)}{\ln2}=0.9223527956\ldots .
\]
