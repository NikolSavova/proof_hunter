# Near-ambient pair stars: the directional rectangle still loses a square root

**Date:** 2026-08-15. All logarithms are base two. This continues
`../agent_shield_circuit_cover/RECOVERABLE_CARRIER_COARSENING_MASK_RUN_GATE.md`
at its few-run endpoint. No arbitrary-child cyclic factorization is used.

## Verdict

The two boundary directions, exact cap/cup energy, and minimizer reflection
do not by themselves supply the missing near-ambient multiplier.

Let an induced child \(X\) have \(m\) labels and \(H=V(X)\) ordinary
faces. In any generic direction its nonsingleton faces have the exact
endpoint decomposition

\[
                     H-m-1=\sum_e C_eU_e.                 \tag{1}
\]

Thus one actual endpoint pair \(e\) has a pair-star

\[
 \mathcal J_e=\{F:e\text{ is the left/right endpoint pair of }F\},
 \qquad J_e=C_eU_e\ge {H-m-1\over\binom m2}.             \tag{2}
\]

If \(A\) left contexts attach to the \(C_e\) cap traces and \(B\) right
contexts attach to the \(U_e\) cup traces in genuine linear seams, the
available ordinary banks have sizes \(AC_e\) and \(BU_e\). The ordered
pair of these two outputs is injective on

\[
                  \mathcal A\times\mathcal J_e\times\mathcal B,
 \qquad\text{so}\qquad V(\Pi)^2\ge ABJ_e.              \tag{3}
\]

This is an exact two-record bank, not a one-face multiplication theorem.
To reach the one-face target \(J_eT\), (3) needs

\[
                              AB\ge J_eT^2,              \tag{4}
\]

which is vastly stronger than the natural context product \(AB\asymp T\).
Reflection minimality has the wrong sign for repairing this. If the only
chart-dependent contribution is \(AC_e+BU_e\), comparison with the
reflected child gives

\[
                         (A-B)(C_e-U_e)\le0.             \tag{5}
\]

Thus the larger outside demand is paired with the smaller directional
profile. Replacement minimizes the same weighted support functional; it
does not lower-bound it. This is the endpoint-star specialization of
`../agent_common_shield_mixing/MINIMIZER_COMMON_GUARD_PROFILE_MUTATIONS.md`.

There is a scalable rational trace regression at exactly the
near-ambient **deficit scale**. Choose **any** rational realization of an \(m\)-point
child, in particular an actual \(m\)-point minimizer, and projectively put
its labels in one strict nesting chain between two convex outer arcs.
For the endpoint star in (2), name its outer and inner labels \(o,p\).
Then

\[
                    p\in\operatorname{int}\triangle(o,a,b)       \tag{6}
\]

for every left label \(a\) and right label \(b\). Hence the load-one
compatible incidence set from the run gate satisfies

\[
 E=\{(F,S)\in\mathcal J_e\times\mathcal P:
              F\cup S\text{ ordinary}\}=\varnothing             \tag{7}
\]

whenever every \(S\in\mathcal P\) meets both arcs.

Let each side have \(P\) selected nonempty partial-transversal states. Even
granting **every** child face to either one-sided context, the complete
output family generated by these trace states has

\[
             V_{\rm tr}\le H(1+2P)+(m+1)P^2.            \tag{8}
\]

For the near-ambient deficit

\[
 m\asymp {n\over\log\log n},\qquad
 \Delta=\Phi(\log n)-\Phi(\log m)
          =\Theta((\log n)\log\log\log n),              \tag{9}
\]

put \(T=2^\Delta\) and choose \(P=2^{\Delta/2+o(\Delta)}\). Then

\[
 J_eP^2\ge {HT\over n^{O(1)}}                         \tag{10}
\]

is a target-sized record rectangle up to polynomial loss, but

\[
                    V_{\rm tr}\le 4H\sqrt T             \tag{11}
\]

for all sufficiently large \(n\). It misses the required \(HT\) by
\(\sqrt T=n^{\Theta(\log\log\log n)}\). The common \(1+3\) circuit
complement gives the \(P^2=T\) base faces, while the child gives the
\(J_e\) faces. Their joint tag is precisely the separated pair counted in
(3); it does not create a mixed ordinary face.

This is a sharp **local deficit-scale gate**, not a live-normalized
Erdős-838
counterexample. The child can be induction-minimal and the selected record
rectangle is of the required scale, but the assembled ambient order type
is not proved globally minimizing and additional multi-label role faces
may pay. Therefore the only remaining positive escape is genuinely
nonlocal: an ambient mutation/composition theorem producing a new mixed
face, or a globally bounded decoder which retains information from both
the child face and the two-sided context. Child minimality, reflection,
endpoint energy, and the corrected linear-profile potential alone do not
provide it. In particular, the \(P^2=2^{O(L L_3)}\) outside contexts used
below cannot carry coefficient-half canonical source mass with bounded
projection load. The exact correction is proved in
`NEAR_AMBIENT_LIVE_CONTEXT_COEFFICIENT_AUDIT.md`.

## 1. Exact endpoint star and the two-output decoder

Fix a generic horizontal direction on \(X\). Every nonsingleton ordinary
face \(F\) has unique leftmost and rightmost labels \(e=(u,v)\). Its upper
hull chain is a cap in \(\mathcal C_e\), and its lower hull chain is a cup
in \(\mathcal U_e\). Conversely, the union of any
\((C,U)\in\mathcal C_e\times\mathcal U_e\) is ordinary and has endpoint
pair \(e\). The two chains recover their union. Therefore

\[
              \mathcal J_e\cong\mathcal C_e\times\mathcal U_e,
 \qquad |\mathcal J_e|=C_eU_e,                                  \tag{12}
\]

and summing (12) proves (1)--(2).

Suppose now that all unions \(S_L\cup C\), with
\(S_L\in\mathcal A,C\in\mathcal C_e\), and all unions
\(U\cup S_R\), with \(U\in\mathcal U_e,S_R\in\mathcal B\), are
ordinary. This is exactly what a left/right linear strong seam gives; it
is not asserted for an arbitrary cyclic gap. The map

\[
 (S_L,C,U,S_R)\longmapsto
              (S_L\cup C,\ U\cup S_R)                            \tag{13}
\]

is injective because the disjoint labelled grounds recover all four
traces. This proves (3).

There are three numerically distinct possible payments for the target
\(J_eT\):

\[
 AC_e\ge J_eT,\qquad BU_e\ge J_eT,\qquad
 ABJ_e\ge(J_eT)^2.                                               \tag{14}
\]

The third is (4). No averaging turns it into \(AB\ge T\); doing so would
erase one power of the child bank. Even the optimistic one-sided full-face
banks \(AJ_e,BJ_e\) need \(A\ge T\) or \(B\ge T\). The symmetric context
split \(A=B=\sqrt T\) misses by exactly \(\sqrt T\).

## 2. What minimizer reflection actually says

Hold the exterior configuration fixed and suppose a permissible child
reflection swaps \(C_e,U_e\) while leaving every chart-independent term
unchanged. The current and reflected directional contributions are

\[
                         AC_e+BU_e,qquad AU_e+BC_e.               \tag{15}
\]

Minimality of the current chart gives the first no larger than the second,
which is exactly (5). If \(A>B\), it forces \(C_e\le U_e\). This is the
same cup-left/cap-right anti-alignment sign as the exact common-guard
mutation inequality; it is not a balance estimate.

More generally, replacement by a chart \(\theta\) imposes

\[
                AC_e+BU_e\le AC_e(\theta)+BU_e(\theta).           \tag{16}
\]

It selects a lower exposed point of the child's directional-profile
polytope. A lower bound on (16) would require a new all-directions theorem.
The identity \(C_eU_e=J_e\) alone only gives
\(AC_e+BU_e\ge2\sqrt{ABJ_e}\), again the two-record square scale.

The corrected linear-profile potential does not alter this conclusion.
If its downward-potential hypothesis occurs in an actual ordered
strong-glue chart, its first-cap/last-cup bank is valid and may close the
branch. In the present trace regression every such one-sided output is
already included in the deliberately generous terms \(HP\) of (8), while
every output crossing both arcs has child rank at most one and is included
in \((m+1)P^2\). Hence the potential theorem can close this trace only if
one of its hypotheses forces a face forbidden by (6); reflection and
endpoint energy do not force that hypothesis. The coherent-ramp survivor
therefore remains an applicability gate, not a proved planar construction.
This is exactly the stopping condition in
`../agent_shield_circuit_cover/FIXED_GAP_LINEAR_PROFILE_POTENTIAL.md`, not
a counterexample to its positive drop theorem.

## 3. Projectively universal central nesting

Normalize limiting arc points as \(u=(-1,0),v=(1,0)\). For a point below
\(uv\), use positive tangent coordinates

\[
 L(z)={1+x\over-y},\qquad R(z)={1-x\over-y}.                       \tag{17}
\]

Their inverse is projective, and

\[
 z\in\operatorname{int}\triangle(u,v,z')
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L(z)>L(z'),\ R(z)>R(z').     \tag{18}
\]

For any rational realization \(X_0=\{(f_i,g_i)\}\), take sufficiently
small rational \(\varepsilon>0\) and set

\[
 L_i=L_0+\varepsilon f_i+\varepsilon^2g_i,qquad
 R_i=R_0+\varepsilon f_i-\varepsilon^2g_i.                        \tag{19}
\]

After ordering distinct \(f_i\), both sequences are strictly increasing.
The map from \((f,g)\) through \((L,R)\) to the plane is projective with a
fixed-sign denominator, so it preserves the complete labelled order type
of \(X_0\). Shrinking two rational convex macro arcs about \(u,v\) makes
(18) hold strictly for every actual left/right label pair. Thus every
child face and, in particular, the endpoint star from (2) survives, while
(6)--(7) hold.

Classify a restricted face by whether its outer support meets neither arc,
only the left arc, only the right arc, or both. The first class has at most
\(H\) states. Granting all child faces in each one-sided class gives at most
\(HP\) states per side. In the two-sided class, (6) permits at most an empty
or singleton child trace, giving \((m+1)P^2\). This proves (8).

For a common circuit-complement routing, the two-sided base state itself is
ordinary and there are \(P^2\) such states. It forgets \(F\) and therefore
has exact uniform load \(J_e\). Allowing an empty or singleton child trace
gives at most \((m+1)P^2\) states and average load at least
\(J_e/(m+1)\). Retaining \(F\) separately makes the output pair injective,
but returns only (3).

## 4. Near-ambient coefficient audit

Write

\[
 L=\log n,\qquad L_2=\log L,\qquad L_3=\log L_2,
 \qquad a=\log(n/m)=L_3+O(1).                                   \tag{20}
\]

For the pure half target \(\Phi(L)=L^2/2\),

\[
                  \Delta=La-a^2/2.                              \tag{21}
\]

The usual correction \(-CL\log L\) changes this by only
\(O(aL_2+a^2)=o(La)\). Take an alphabet
\(D=\Theta(n/L)\) and
\(k=\lceil\Delta/(2\log(D+1))\rceil=(1/2+o(1))a\) active
roles on each outer side. Their partial transversals contain a selected
subfamily of size

\[
 P=\lfloor2^{\Delta/2}\rfloor=\sqrt T\,(1-o(1)).                 \tag{22}
\]

Only \(O(nL_3/L)=o(n)\) active outer labels are needed, so this trace fits
inside the canonical point budget; unused ambient labels are not claimed
to be harmless.

By induction, \(H\ge2^{\Phi(L-a)}\), and hence
\(H\gg m\sqrt T\). Equations (8) and (22) give (11). Meanwhile (2) gives

\[
                 J_eP^2\ge {H-m-1\over\binom m2}T
                         ={HT\over n^{O(1)}},                    \tag{23}
\]

proving (10). Thus the failure occurs at the exact demand scale, not
because too few formal records were supplied.

What (23) does **not** prove is that these records occur with live canonical
marked weight in one global minimizer. Indeed, before pocket replication
their source marginal is controlled by \(P^2\), not by \(J_eP^2\); the
live-context audit cited above closes the bounded-projection-load case.
The projective construction proves
only that no theorem based on the listed local invariants can manufacture
their missing mixed output. A full positive proof may still use ambient
minimality to rule out the surrounding nesting or to charge additional
faces outside the restricted trace complex.
