## 2. What was accomplished in the latest attack

For a distance-Sidon set `A` in an `m` by `m` grid, put

\[
 D=A-A,\qquad N=|D|,\qquad S=|D+D|,
\]

and let `J` be quarter-turn.  The earlier reductions show that the grid
upper bound follows from the adaptive off-diagonal estimate

\[
 \mathcal O_K\le N^{1+o(1)}S.                 \tag{2.1}
\]

The last several hours replaced a loose cross-fibre charge first by an exact
two-anchor graph and then by a swap-coupled six-anchor charge.

For each normal seven-incidence configuration, endpoint realization gives

\[
 \omega=(\epsilon(c),m(u)-m(c)),\qquad
 \ell=w-(I+J)q,\qquad z=w-q.
\]

For fixed `omega`, make a bipartite multigraph `G_omega` with an edge
`(ell,z)` for every configuration.  Orient each edge toward its endpoint of
smaller global degree, with a fixed tie rule.  This charge is injective
inside every rich fibre.  Together with two exact common-endpoint routes it
lands in at most `10NS` labelled keys.

The decisive exact inequality is

\[
 \sum\lambda_N^2\le
 \mathcal B_N:=\sum_\omega\sum_{e=(\ell,z)\in G_\omega}
 \min\{d_L(\ell),d_R(z)\}.                    \tag{2.2}
\]

This is not an estimate: it follows directly from the orientation.  It
removes the one-sided star obstruction which defeated the prior charge.
Any large normal second moment must now contain many edges with both anchor
degrees large in the same endpoint-midpoint graph.

The two fixed-anchor collision systems are also exact.  If two preimages
differ by `(eta,rho,pi)` in their base and two popular shifts, then the
left-anchor displacements are

\[
 \eta,\ \eta+\rho,\ \eta+\pi,\ 0,\ J\rho,
 (I+J)\rho-\pi,\ (I+J)(\rho-\pi),              \tag{2.3}
\]

and the right-anchor displacements are

\[
 \eta,\ \eta+\rho,\ \eta+\pi,\ 0,\ \rho-\pi,
 -J\rho,\ \rho-(I+J)\pi.                      \tag{2.4}
\]

The common endpoint decoration forces `eta`; it is not a free affine
variable.  Both adaptive-popular shifts remain present.

Every ordered configuration `(q,p)` also contains its swapped partner
`(p,q)`.  Retaining both endpoint labels produces four `w`-anchor cells,
and the two moving heads produce two more cells.  Charging to the least
popular of these six candidates gives a new exact envelope

\[
 \sum\lambda_6^2\le\mathcal B_6\le\mathcal B_N.               \tag{2.5}
\]

The complete charge remains fibrewise injective and now lands in at most
`18NS` labelled keys.  The larger constant is irrelevant; the substantial
gain is that a counterexample must be simultaneously dense under the swap
involution, both endpoint labels, four paired anchors, and two heads.

## 3. The exact theorem still missing

The strongest current normal target is

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal B_6\le N^{o(1)}|C_N|.}        \tag{3.1}
\]

where `C_N` is the multiset of normal configurations.  The two
common-endpoint charge routes need the analogous size-biased load bound.
Together these imply

\[
 \sum\lambda^2\le N^{o(1)}\sum\lambda,
\]

and Cauchy in an `18NS` target gives (2.1), hence

\[
 |A|\le m^{2/3+o(1)}
\]

and the full expected upper order for #1208.

No proof of (3.1) is known.  This is the honest boundary between the work
completed and the open problem.  The older sufficient target for `B_N` and
its arboricity reduction remain valid fallbacks, since `B_6<=B_N`, but they
do not exploit the new swap coupling.

For the older target there is a sharper sufficient gate.  Let `a(G_omega)` be
the multigraph arboricity.  Orienting a forest decomposition gives

\[
 \mathcal B_N\le
 2\sum_\omega a(G_\omega)|E(G_\omega)|.         \tag{3.2}
\]

Hence subpolynomial size-biased arboricity still suffices.  Moreover,
if `r_{ell,z}` is the parallel-edge multiplicity, then exactly

\[
 \mathcal B_N=
 \sum_{\omega,\ell,z}r_{\ell,z}^2+\mathcal M_3, \tag{3.3}
\]

where `M_3` is a selectable family of genuine three-edge paths, with one
nonparallel continuation at each endpoint of a central edge.  Read
`BALANCED_ANCHOR_ARBORICITY_GATE.md` for the proof and exact path forms.

## 5. Other durable progress and barriers

The direct attack has accumulated substantially more than the final gate:

- Complete rectangular, Gaussian-ideal, and arbitrary oblique lattice
  fibres were classified.  The sharp universal height threshold is
  `M >> r^(3/2)`, exactly the exponent needed at the cube-root scale.
- Sparse oblique shear examples show that even near-full density, three
  affine fibre copies, radial uniqueness, and adaptive bidirectional
  popularity do not recover a polynomial complete patch.  Complete
  difference endpoint realization is indispensable.
- The orthogonal-energy problem was localized to a support-adaptive rich
  tail.  High-support Ruzsa branches, collinear branches, algebraic-curve
  branches, generic affine images, and large resonance-index branches were
  separated or closed.
- Opposite-endpoint charges, exact popular-pair rectangles, an eight-form
  fractional-basis collision majorant, and endpoint-switched two-moment
  charges remain valid fallback interfaces.
- The common-endpoint fallback is not arbitrary: a repeated local midpoint
  class forces three affine copies in `A` and squared-distance ratio
  `1:1/4:5/4`.
- Generic hypergraph codegrees, anti-Ramsey proper coloring, raw distance
  energy, DRC, additive BSG, ordinary third energy, two-sided sumset
  products, scalar Bessel positivity, full directional PSD relaxations,
  and density-to-complete-patch extraction were all shown to bottom out at
  the grid or admit explicit countermodels.  Do not restart these lanes
  without a new endpoint-sensitive ingredient.
- The prime-power upper framework has a local entropy barrier and will not
  reach exponent `1/3`; its value is the separate explicit partial result
  `0.494586`.

## 6. Best next move

Work directly on (3.1), not on another one-sided charge redesign.

1. Dyadically stratify configurations by the minimum of all available six
   cell degrees, not only the two degrees in one `G_omega`.
2. Retain the swap involution `(q,p)<->(p,q)`.  A bad stratum is dense in
   two endpoint labels, four `w`-anchor projections, and two head
   projections simultaneously; discarding any one of these returns to a
   known countermodel.
3. Use `z_q-ell_q=Jq` and `z_p-ell_p=Jp`; the two anchor pairs recover both
   popular shifts.  Combine this with the endpoint switches in both
   `omega_p` and `omega_q` rather than treating either graph abstractly.
4. Use the fixed-head collision system from
   `SWAP_COUPLED_SIX_ANCHOR_CHARGE.md`, whose seven displacements are
   `-rho,0,pi-rho,kappa-rho,kappa-pi,kappa-Lrho,kappa-Lpi`.
5. The old Nash--Williams/arboricity and parallel-path decomposition remain
   available inside each endpoint graph, but now serve as a preliminary
   extraction rather than the final theorem.
6. Treat the common-endpoint singleton and resonance routes separately;
   no stored stress activates the resonance route, but there is not yet a
   theorem that it is empty.

The practical progress estimate is: the construction/verification
architecture is mature, and the full conjecture is reduced to one crisp
new combinatorial inequality, but that inequality is of genuinely
open-problem strength.  We are meaningfully closer to a proof route, not
close enough to predict completion.
