## Class/ray coordinates

Let `R0` be the first nonprincipal odd prime ideal.  It has norm 11 and residue
root 4.  If `(h)=R0^2`, the exact ray columns are

\[
u_1=1,\qquad u_2=13,\qquad h=12
\]

in the four-dimensional binary sign/mod-4 ray quotient.  In particular

\[
h=u_1+u_2,
\qquad
H=\langle u_1,u_2,h\rangle=\{0,1,12,13\}.
\]

Thus the residual quotient `W/H` is two-dimensional, with canonical colors
`{0,2,4,6}`.

For a principal prime ideal `P`, choose a generator of `P` and call its
quotient color `a_P`.  For a nonprincipal `P`, choose a generator of `P R0`
and call its color `b_P`.  These colors are independent of generator choices
modulo `H`.

### No selected nonprincipal ideal

Then `Cl_S[2]=C_2` survives.  Besides the two global units and the `T`
principal generators, the unramified class generator `h` gives pre-ray
dimension `T+3`.  The residual constraint image is generated by the selected
`a_P` colors.  If their quotient span has dimension `e`, then

\[
d=T+3-(2+e).
\]

### At least one selected nonprincipal ideal

Now the selected set kills the class group and the pre-ray dimension is
`T+2`.  Taking any selected nonprincipal `R` as reference, the residual
constraint image is generated by

\[
\{a_P:P\text{ principal}\}
\cup
\{b_P+b_R:P\ne R\text{ nonprincipal}\}.
\]

This expression is independent of the chosen reference.  If its span has
dimension `e`, then

\[
d=T+2-(2+e).
\]

Every span of principal colors is a subspace `L` of the two-dimensional
quotient, and all selected nonprincipal colors must lie in one affine coset of
`L`.  The quotient has exactly five subspaces; they yield five all-principal
cells and eleven nonprincipal affine-coset cells.  This proves that the 16
enumerated cases are exhaustive, rather than a heuristic color sample.

## Square, fourth, and uncapped inertia

For each attainable `215<=d<=218`, let

\[
R_{\max}=\left\lfloor\frac{d^2-1}{4}\right\rfloor.
\]

If `s` inertia generators receive square caps, the optimistic number of useful
quadratic Frobenius caps is

\[
N=R_{\max}-(d+1)-s.
\]

For every `0<=f<=217-s`, the verifier checks exactly at `x=2/d` that

\[
1-dx+R_{\max}x^2+f x^4<0.
\]

An uncapped inertia generator and a fourth-power-capped generator use the same
quadratic relation budget.  Replacing the uncapped generator by a fourth cap
lowers its asymptotic root-discriminant exponent from `1/2` to `3/8`, while the
displayed inequality proves that the extra quartic relator remains admissible.
Hence every uncapped cell is strictly dominated by a square/fourth cell.

It remains to enumerate `f=0,...,217`, with the other inertia generators
square-capped.  The optimistic assignment puts fourth caps on the smallest
selected ideals and adds the first `f` remaining ideals as useful.  All formal
ideals are declared useful, so exclusions do not rely on any missing CM
residue test.
