## Exact `C_4` class/ray coordinates

The certified arithmetic gives

\[
\operatorname{Cl}(K)\simeq C_4,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cl}^+(K)\simeq C_4\times C_2.
\]

Let `R` be the first prime ideal whose class generates `C_4`.  It is the
second ideal in norm order, of norm 13 and residue root 6.  Normalize its
class to one.  If `(h)=R^4`, the exact ray columns in the four-dimensional
binary sign/mod-4 quotient are

\[
u_1=12,\qquad u_2=13,\qquad h=12.
\]

Thus

\[
B=\langle u_1,u_2,h\rangle=\{0,1,12,13\}
\]

has dimension two, and the residual quotient is `F_2^2`, represented by
colors `{0,2,4,6}`.

For a prime ideal `P` of normalized class `a in Z/4Z`, choose a generator
`z_P` of

\[

\]

where the exponent is represented modulo four.  Its ray column modulo `B` is
the color `c_P`.  Changing any chosen generator changes the column only by an
element of `B`, so the quotient color is intrinsic.

Then the localized class group still has one nontrivial order-two class.  A
squareclass basis consists of the two global units, `h`, and one `z_P` for

### At least one odd class label is selected

Any odd class generates `C_4`, so the localized class group is trivial.  Fix
one selected odd-class ideal `P_0`.  A squareclass basis consists of the two
global units, `h`, every even-class `z_P`, and the products
`z_P z_{P_0}` for the other odd-class ideals.  The pre-ray dimension is
`T+2`, and the residual ray image is generated by

\[
\{c_P:P\text{ even class}\}
\cup
\{c_P+c_{P_0}:P\ne P_0\text{ odd class}\}.
\]

The expression is independent of the odd reference.  Therefore all selected
even colors lie in a subspace `L`, while every selected odd color lies in one
affine coset of `L`.  If `dim L=e`, then

\[
d=T+2-(2+e).
\]

The residual plane has five subspaces.  They produce five all-even cells and
eleven cells consisting of a subspace for the even colors and an affine coset
for the odd colors.  Hence the 16 enumerated cells exhaust the `C_4`
class/ray possibilities.

\[

\[
