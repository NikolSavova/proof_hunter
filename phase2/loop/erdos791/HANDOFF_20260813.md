## 0. Executive state

**The problem is not solved.  No numerical record was improved.**

The deepest new fact from the latest attack is that the earlier chromatic
barrier is not fundamental:

> For every fixed `r`, there are `r` elementary tiles of size `t+O_r(1)` in
> a block of length `t^2` such that every distinct pair covers all residues
> modulo `t^2`.

Every pair supplies a stationary carry self-loop.  Hence efficient
point-footprint languages can contain `K_r` for arbitrarily large fixed `r`.
For `r=7`, the Turán threshold `1/2-1/(4r)=13/28` already exceeds the known
upper endpoint `0.4585`.

The decisive remaining obstruction is **temporal compatibility**.  Adjacent
targets generally have representations using different role pairs, whereas
the proved tiles reliably transition only from a pair state to itself.  For
the optimized seven-role family at `t=251`, only 25 of 441 ordered state
transitions survive: 21 self-loops and four cross transitions.  At the tested
scales `t=271,301,331,421`, exactly the 21 self-loops remain.

So the next solution-level lemma is no longer “find seven efficient roles.”
It must do one of the following:

1. assign representations of a limsup-extremal basis so that role-pair states
   have long compatible runs with only `o(k)` duplication;
2. construct efficient tiles with a genuinely mixing cross-state automaton;
3. show all transition holes lie in additive rectangles `U+V` with
   `|U|+|V|=o(k)`, then invoke the proved absorber; or
4. bypass amplifiers and prove a universal upper bound matching a scalable
   lower construction.

The first and third options currently look most mathematically concrete.

## 1. Problem, notation, and exact logical target

Let

```text
g(N)=min{|A|: A subset Z_{≥0}, [0,N] subset A+A},
R(k)=max{N: some |A|<=k has [0,N] subset A+A}.
```

Write

```text
alpha_-=liminf R(k)/k^2,
alpha_+=limsup R(k)/k^2.
```

The original limit question is equivalent to `alpha_-=alpha_+`.  Exact
inverse identities proved in `closure/theory/STATUS_AND_THEORY.md` are

```text
liminf g(N)^2/N = 1/alpha_+,
limsup g(N)^2/N = 1/alpha_-.
```

Therefore a better finite/scalable construction improves `alpha_-` but does
**not** by itself settle existence of the limit.  A full solution requires a
liminf/limsup bridge or matching universal upper and lower bounds.

The audited published window is

```text
85/294 <= alpha_- <= alpha_+ <= 0.4585.
```

Equivalently,

```text
2.181025... <= liminf g(N)^2/N
             <= limsup g(N)^2/N <= 294/85=3.4588235....
```

Kohonen 2017 supplies the construction-side constant `85/294`.  Yu 2015
supplies the analytic endpoint `0.4585`.  Faust--Tait 2025 explicitly retain
the limit-existence question, and Nathanson 2026 still describes the
asymptotic problem as open.  No post-2017 improvement to Kohonen's interval
construction was found in the primary-source kill-search.

## 2. The original Kohonen/Mrose certificate lane

For scale `t`, define

```text
V=[0,t],
H=t[0,t-1],
S=(t+1)[0,t-1].
```

For macro placement lists `I,J,K`, put

```text
A_t=(V+t^2 I) union (H+t^2 J) union (S+t^2 K).
```

The exact sufficient square predicate is

```text
q in I+J
or q in I+K
or ({q-1,q} subset J+K).                                (K)
```

The third clause needs **two consecutive** `H+S` parallelograms.  Membership
of `q` in `J+K` alone does not cover a full square.  Predicate (K) is exact
for the certificate language but only sufficient, not necessary, for literal
coverage by `A_t+A_t`.

## 3. Closed construction families and no-go results

These paths were tried hard enough that a new instance should not restart
them without a genuinely different ingredient.

### 3.1 Full hidden Kohonen staircase

The full parametric arithmetic-block family behind Kohonen was identified and
bounded exactly.  Its density satisfies

```text
m/ell^2 <= 85/294,
```

with equality uniquely at Kohonen's parameters.  A more general unbounded
serial staircase, including reflected final blocks, has the same sharp
obstruction.  Millions of finite parameter checks support the symbolic
identity, which is proved algebraically in `closure/tiles/CLOSURE_RESULT.md`.

Consequence: changing block lengths, appending another reflected block, or
serially extending the same coprime-step/dense-block architecture cannot beat
the record.

### 3.2 Products, concatenation, and fixed residues

- Mixed-radix Cartesian products multiply efficiency, so iterating a
  non-perfect finite basis collapses density at additive order two.
- Restricted concatenation grows range only linearly.
- Faust--Tait cyclic digit iteration preserves cardinality efficiency only by
  increasing additive order; it does not amplify at fixed order two.
- A cyclic basis can fail at integer carries; modular coverage is not integer
  initial-interval coverage.
- Retaining every fixed residue class jointly in the profinite limit adds no
  strength: the profinite relaxation constant equals the ordinary continuous
  convolution constant.  Any useful modulus must grow with `k` and retain
  lattice-scale carries/collisions, not aggregate residue mass alone.

### 3.3 Triangle-free component schedulers

If efficient current pair interactions form a triangle-free graph, weighted
Mantel gives density at most `1/4`.  This closes role-separated finite-state
cycles and common carry-free mixed-radix boxes.  Beating the record needs
dual-role/same-role interactions or a truly quadratic carry triangle.

An exact interleaved family achieved the sharp `1/4` ceiling:

```text
ell=2k+2h,
m=4kh,
ell^2-4m=4(k-h)^2.
```

Interleaving alone is therefore not enough.

### 3.4 Three-tile universal collision bound

For `a=|I|,b=|J|,c=|K|`, every certificate obeys

```text
m <= ab+ac+bc-min(b,c)
  <= floor(ell^2/3)-floor(ell/3).
```

The bound is sharp for an infinite family.  It improves raw capacity
screening but has leading constant `1/3`, too weak to close the problem.  An
exact parity relaxation has a matching rational primal/SOS dual at `1/3`, so
parity alone is exhausted.

## 4. Analytic hierarchy: exact results and its limitation

### 4.1 Carry-bin flow relaxation

At mesh `m`, let `p_i` be normalized mass in element bin `i`.  Unordered pair
cell `(i,j)` has capacity

```text
p_i p_j    (i<j),
p_i^2/2    (i=j),
```

and may route only to target bins `i+j` and `i+j+1`.  Let `U_m` be the maximum
uniform target demand routable by these flows.  Proved:

```text
alpha_+ <= U_m,
U_(rm) <= U_m,
inf_r U_(2^r)=C_conv,
```

where

```text
C_conv=sup{c: some probability mu on [0,1] has mu*mu>=2c lambda}.
```

The reverse compactness proof requires a real measure construction: trim each
target bin to exact mass, symmetrize off-diagonal flow atoms, dominate fixed
coarse ordered dyadic rectangles after refinement, then use Portmanteau and a
monotone-class argument.  Do not replace this by the false claim that scalar
cell capacities give pointwise within-cell domination.

Exploratory outer optimization gave values down to about `0.45597` at 20
bins.  These are **lower bounds on the nonconvex relaxation optimum**, not
certified upper bounds on `alpha_+`.

### 4.2 Why weak limits do not discretize

Every asymptotic sequence at density `c` has a weak limit `mu` with

```text
mu*mu >= 2c lambda.
```

But constant-mean independent rounding leaves a positive hole density.  In a
diffuse model the expected missed fraction is at least

```text
exp(-1/(2c)),
```

which is `0.177389...` at `c=85/294`.  Any ordinary alteration then needs
`Omega(k)` repair.  This kills independent sample/expected-holes/alter
arguments, not correlated designs.

## 5. Amplifier bridge and role-defect theory

### 5.1 Cross-cover amplifier

If `[0,n] subset V+H` and `L=|V|+|H|`, then

```text
union_{v in V}(vq^2+[0,q-1])
union_{h in H}(hq^2+q[0,q-1])
```

has at most `Lq` elements and covers `[0,(n+1)q^2-1]`.  Therefore

```text
alpha_- >= (n+1)/L^2.
```

If limsup-extremal bases had cross-cover cost `k+o(k)`, the limit would
exist.  This is exact but probably too restrictive: finite Kohonen expansions
show substantial two-role cross-cover defect.

### 5.2 Multitype closure theorem

The stronger exact bridge is:

> If bases with `n_r/k_r^2 -> alpha_+` admit point-footprint/carry
> certificates of total role cost `L_r=k_r+o(k_r)`, then
> `alpha_-=alpha_+`.

The scalable Kohonen expansion has defect one, proving the premise can hold
for a structured family.  It does not approach `alpha_+`.

### 5.3 Unique-sum chromatic obstruction

For a coordinate basis, build the graph whose off-diagonal edge `{a,b}` is a
required target with a unique unordered representation.  Deleting multiply
typed coordinates leaves this graph colorable by the current-role
compatibility graph.  If the latter has chromatic number `r` and role defect
fraction tends to `x`, representation counting and Turán give

```text
2c-1/2 <= (r-1)/(2r)+x/r-x^2/(2r).
```

In particular a near-lossless assignment (`x=0`) forces

```text
c <= 1/2-1/(4r).
```

The earlier five-role language had `r=4`, ceiling `7/16`; reaching the known
upper endpoint required `r>=7`.  The latest `K_r` tiles eliminate every fixed
version of this obstruction.

Finite extremizers through `k=9` are not automatically easy to type: best
defect is `k+4`, and one `k=8` extremizer needs role cost `2k`.  A universal
“only zero needs duplication” statement is false.

## 6. Latest construction: unbounded carry cliques

This is the main new theorem.  Detailed proof:
`full_attack3/automaton/AUTOMATON_RESULT.md`.

Fix `r>=2` and set

```text
M=lcm(1,...,r-1),
D_r={0,M,2M,...,(r-1)M}.
```

For `a<b` in `D_r`, define

```text
d=b-a,
u=a^2/d,
v=b^2/d,
h=ab/d.
```

These are integers because `d=(j-i)M` divides `ab=ijM^2`.  Put

```text
C_r=max_{a<b} max(v,u+v-b).
```

For sufficiently large `t=1 mod M`, in particular `t>max h`, let

```text
B=t^2,
R_a={ [i(t+a)]_B : -C_r<=i<=t+C_r-1 }.
```

Then `|R_a|=t+O_r(1)` and for every distinct pair

```text
R_a+R_b mod B=Z/BZ.                                    (UC)
```

The lattice map

```text
phi(i,j)=i(t+a)+j(t+b) mod B
```

has kernel vectors

```text
p=(t-h,u),
q=(-v,t-b+v),
```

both mapping to zero modulo `B`, with determinant `B`.  Surjectivity must be
stated: `t=1 mod M` makes a nonzero `t+a` a unit modulo `t^2` (if `a=0`, use
`t+b`), so the kernel has index `B`; hence `p,q` form the full kernel.  Their
fundamental parallelogram lies in the endpoint-padded index box.

For the optimized seven-slope set

```text
D={0,10,12,15,20,30,60},
C=120,
```

the same proof holds for `t>60`, `gcd(t,30)=1`, and adequate no-repeat size.
This much smaller instance is symbolically and literally checked.

### What modular coverage actually gives

For pair state `e={a,b}`, split the raw integer sumset into lower and carry
masks:

```text
L_e=(R_a+R_b) intersect [0,B-1],
U_e=((R_a+R_b) intersect [B,2B-1])-B.
```

(UC) is exactly

```text
L_e union U_e=[0,B-1].
```

Thus two consecutive macro occurrences of the **same state** cover a block:
the occurrence at `s-1` supplies `U_e`, and the occurrence at `s` supplies
`L_e`.  A transition from old state `e` to new state `f` instead requires

```text
U_e union L_f=[0,B-1].                                 (T)
```

(UC) does not imply (T).  This compatibility/transition distinction is the
central lesson of attack 3.

### Limiting/interpolation detail

A construction at admissible scales proves a liminf bound only after bounded-
gap interpolation.  For an arbitrary cardinality budget `k`, choose the
largest admissible `t` with `L(t+2C_r)<=k`; admissible scales contain the fixed
progression `1 mod M`, so `t=k/L+O(1)`.  Then a certificate of `m` macro
blocks proves

```text
R(k)/k^2 >= (mt^2-1)/k^2 -> m/L^2.
```

Do not say merely “let `t` tend to infinity,” which proves only a subsequence
statement without this interpolation.

## 7. Carry triangle and approximate-tile theory

### 7.1 Exact projective carry triangle

For `q>=3`, put

```text
B=q^2-q+1,
X=(q-1)[0,q-1],
Y=[0,q-1],
Z={0} union {1+qj:0<=j<=q-2}.
```

All tiles have size `q`, and

```text
X+Y=Y+Z=[0,B-1],
X+Z mod B=Z/BZ.
```

Consecutive `XZ` macro sums cover a block via complementary carry masks.  The
local overhead is asymptotically zero.  However, for every `q>=4`, the exact
state graph is

```text
XY -> XY,YZ
YZ -> XY,YZ
XZ -> XY,YZ,XZ.
```

A direct state cannot enter `XZ` because the lower `XZ` mask omits 2.  This
is a one-way trap.  Independent translations give all nine transitions only
at exceptional `q=4`; exhaustive `q=5,6` gives at most seven.

### 7.2 Raw approximate triangle no-go

Three `q+o(q)` integer tiles cannot have all three raw pair sums cover
`q^2-o(q^2)` points in intervals of length `q^2`.  Pair counting forces their
normalized convolutions near translated interval-uniform laws.  Centering and
compactness give

```text
mu_1*mu_2=mu_1*mu_3=mu_2*mu_3=Unif[-1/2,1/2].
```

The compactly supported characteristic functions must agree and square to
`2 sin(z/2)/z`, impossible because the latter has simple nonzero zeros while
an entire square has even-multiplicity zeros.

This theorem is deliberately scoped to **one raw pair footprint per block**.
The projective triangle is not a counterexample: its `X+Z` raw lower window
misses `binom(q-2,2)` points, and its best translated `B`-window misses
`floor((q-2)^2/4)`, both positive fractions.  It escapes only through carry.

### 7.3 Exact microtile factorization

If two `q`-element integer tiles cover `q^2` consecutive values, pair count
forces an exact unique factorization of `1+z+...+z^(q^2-1)`.  Squarefreeness
over `Q` gives each normalized tile a unique complementary partner and no
self-partner; normalized exact interaction graphs are matchings.  For prime
`q`, the factors are the standard fine interval/coarse progression pair.

This no-go does not cover cyclic carry splits, `q+O(1)` slack, unions of
partial pair states, or changing tiles with carry state.

## 8. Rank-one absorber: the cleanest positive analytic bridge

If holes `H` of a preliminary basis lie in an additive rectangle `U+V`, then
adjoining `U union V` repairs every hole at cost `|U|+|V|`.

For block length `Q`, if

```text
H subset {Qj+r: j in S, r in R},
```

the cost is `|S|+|R|`, even if every block is bad.  Choosing `Q=q^2` with
`sqrt(k)<<q<<k` makes the number of block starts `O(k^2/q^2)=o(k)`; a common
residual set of size `o(k)` is then harmless.

Exact conditional closure theorem:

> If preliminary sets of size at most `k` and range
> `N_k/k^2 -> alpha_+` leave holes contained in `U_k+V_k` with
> `|U_k|+|V_k|=o(k)`, then `alpha_-=alpha_+`.

This suggests optimizing transition residuals by **additive rank**, not just
by cardinality.  A residual of positive density may still be repairable if it
has a shared rectangle description; conversely `o(t^2)` scattered holes can
still be fatal.

## 10. Best next attacks, in priority order

### A. Temporal representation/role-assignment theorem

This is the most direct route to a full solution.

Given a basis `C` covering `[0,n]`, each target has a set of unordered
representation edges.  One wants to choose representations and assign a
small number of roles to coordinates so that the resulting pair-state
sequence is accepted by the carry automaton, with only `o(|C|)` duplicated
roles.

Useful reformulations to pursue:

1. **Minimum-switch representation path.**  For each target layer `q`, nodes
   are representations `{a,b}` of `q`.  Join layers `q-1,q` according to
   whether their eventual role-pair states can transition.  Prove a path with
   `o(k)` exceptional layers for limsup-extremal bases.
2. **Long cross-sum intervals.**  Show an extremal basis has a partition into
   finitely many sets `P_i` such that most targets lie in long consecutive
   runs of one `P_i+P_j`.  The self-loop amplifier would then apply directly.
3. **Representation multiplicity absorption.**  Dense target layers have
   alternative representations.  Use them to avoid pair-state switches;
   charge unavoidable switches to uniquely represented sums.  The existing
   unique-sum/Turán theorem is the natural starting point, but now the charge
   must be temporal rather than purely chromatic.
4. **Interval graph/dynamic programming experiments.**  On all exact
   extremizers through the next feasible `k`, optimize simultaneously over
   representation choice, coordinate roles, and transition legality.  Mine a
   structural invariant from positive instances.  The previous role-only CP
   models discard exactly the temporal information now known to matter.

Potential proof ingredients: edge-coloring of layered representation
hypergraphs, path-cover/regularity arguments, removal lemmas for switch
layers, additive energy, and a stability theorem saying an extremal basis
cannot have linearly many isolated changes of representation type.

### B. Optimize transition residuals for additive rank

Do not optimize only the count of legal transitions.  For each pair of states
`e,f`, compute

```text
R_ef=[0,B-1] \ (U_e union L_f).
```

Search for tile phases/lifts such that all `R_ef` lie in a small common set,
or admit factorizations `R_ef subset U_ef+V_ef` whose total side cost is
`o(t)`.  Then global switch holes may be absorbed.

Concrete variants:

- translate/reflect each `R_a` independently and optimize the union or
  rectangle rank of all residuals;
- allow two phase variants per role, switching variants rather than roles;
- use unions of two or three pair events as automaton states;
- replace the canonical integer representatives of the modular tiles by
  carefully chosen lifts with the same residues but different carry masks;
- use a slowly growing collection of lift phases and ask whether overhead
  stays `o(t)`;
- optimize shared residuals, not all-to-all exact coverage.

The latest `K_r` slopes solve modular compatibility, so the freedom now lies
almost entirely in choosing integer lifts/carry masks.

### C. Carry-rectangle lemma from correlated rounding

The continuous/profinite relaxations are complete only for aggregate mass.
Try a correlated discretization whose holes are not few but have low additive
rank.  Candidate mechanisms:

- dependent rounding by blocks sharing one residue defect;
- algebraic designs/orthogonal arrays tied to carry position;
- randomized shifts followed by selecting the best small family of shifts;
- nibble/absorption with a deterministic common residual template;
- difference-aware rather than residue-only finite hierarchies.

The success criterion is exactly an `o(k)` additive rectangle, not vanishing
hole density.  Independent point sampling is rigorously dead.

### D. Universal upper bound route

The carry-bin hierarchy could in principle produce a certified finite-level
dual bound.  A match to a scalable construction would determine the constant
and solve the limit.  Current heuristic `U_m` values are far above
`85/294`, so this does not presently look close.  Any exact outer dual needs
copositive/SOS or interval arithmetic and must be a true upper certificate;
the existing optimizer reports feasible lower values of `U_m` only.

### E. Numerical-record side quest

Global SAT/CP search for `(42,511)` or smaller record-beating certificates is
still worthwhile as a publishable construction result and may reveal new
geometry.  It should be treated as a side quest, because it does not answer
the limit question without a closure theorem.

## 11. Speculative observations not yet theorems

These are ideas worth retaining but must not be stated as results.

1. The unbounded `K_r` family has `M=lcm(1,...,r-1)` and phase radius roughly
   exponential in `r`.  One might let `r=r(t)` grow very slowly while keeping
   `C_r=o(t)`.  This would remove even slowly growing chromatic requirements,
   but it does nothing by itself about cross-state transitions.
2. The collapse to self-loops may be a property of the canonical lifts, not
   of the modular clique.  Different representatives of the same residues
   can drastically alter carry masks.  A classification or optimization of
   lift-induced masks is high value.
3. Exact no-slack cycles force nested/equal lower masks, but redundant
   `t+O(1)` tiles or union states can support nonconstant cycles.  The known
   `H-S-T` triangle is proof that overlap can be amortized across states.
4. The finite projective plane/cyclic difference-set instinct was useful but
   naive affine lines in `Z/p^2Z` fail because integer carries destroy
   coordinatewise `F_p^2` addition.  Work in a cyclic group first, then audit
   integer lift masks explicitly.
5. A finite-state automaton with fixed positive-density cross-state residuals
   may still be useful if all residuals share an additive rectangle.  Do not
   infer failure solely from thousands of holes in one block.
6. Conversely, a highly chromatic direct compatibility graph is irrelevant
   unless its allowed state graph has useful SCCs.  The proof-certified
   finite `chi=7` core and the scalable `K_r` family both demonstrate this.
7. The existence of the limit may follow from a general near-superadditivity
   or thermodynamic-limit principle for optimal bases, but ordinary product
   composition loses efficiency.  Any such principle must be carry-aware
   and nearly cardinality-additive; no candidate inequality is currently
   proved.

3. **Seed normalization:** swap `J/K` consistently before radius constraints.
4. **UNKNOWN:** never call a timed-out SAT/CP run negative evidence stronger
   than a scoped diagnostic.
5. **Continuous flows:** scalar bin capacities do not give pointwise
   within-cell product domination; use the dyadic weak-limit proof.
6. **Turan numbers:** the simple floor `(r-1)N^2/(2r)` is only an upper bound
   in general; the exact finite value is the balanced `r`-partite edge count.
7. **Raw versus cyclic:** modular coverage can split over lower/carry blocks;
   it need not cover one raw interval.
8. **Compatibility versus transition:** `L_e union U_e=Q` proves only
   `e->e`, not `e->f`.
9. **Kernel proof:** determinant `B` gives the full kernel basis only after
   proving `phi` is onto and its kernel has index `B`.
10. **Admissible scales:** for the general `K_r` proof require `t>max h`, not
    merely `t>max slope`.
11. **Phase radius:** the exact general bound is
    `C_r=max(v,u+v-b)`, without the superseded `+1`.
12. **Liminf interpolation:** a construction sequence needs bounded-gap scale
    interpolation.
13. **Approximate triangle theorem:** it covers raw interval footprints only;
    the projective carry triangle lies outside its hypotheses.
14. **Alzahrani:** record the proof defect as an audit finding, not as a
    formally published retraction.
15. **Novelty:** the `K_r` construction has an internal correctness audit but
