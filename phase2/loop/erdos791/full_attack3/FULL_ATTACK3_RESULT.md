# Erdős #791: third full-problem attack

Date: 2026-08-13

## Verdict

The limit problem is **not resolved**, and the published numerical bounds are
unchanged.  This attack does, however, clear the most concrete obstruction
left by the preceding round: asymptotically lossless point-footprint carry
languages can have chromatic number seven, and in fact arbitrarily large
finite chromatic number.

For

```text
R(k)=max{n: some |A|<=k has [0,n] subset A+A},
alpha_-=liminf R(k)/k^2,  alpha_+=limsup R(k)/k^2,
```

the principal new construction is:

> For every fixed `r`, there are `r` elementary tiles of size `t+O_r(1)` in
> a block of length `t^2` such that every two distinct tile types cover all
> residues modulo `t^2`.

Each pair therefore has an exact carry self-loop: occurrences of the same
role pair at consecutive macro sums cover the intervening literal square.
For `r=7`, the corresponding current-role graph contains `K7`, whose
near-lossless Turán threshold is

```text
1/2-1/(4*7)=13/28=0.464285...,
```

above the known upper endpoint `0.4585`.  Thus the earlier chromatic counting
obstruction is genuinely escaped.

The construction is not a complete amplifier.  A pair state generally does
not transition to a different pair state.  The exact seven-role audit has all
21 self-loops but only 25 of 441 ordered transitions at `t=251`.  No theorem
assigns a limsup-extremal basis to finitely many roles so that consecutive
targets retain one role pair.  That temporal representation problem, rather
than the existence of efficient elementary tiles, is now the decisive gap.

## 1. Unbounded family of efficient carry cliques

Fix `r>=2`, put

```text
M=lcm(1,2,...,r-1),
D_r={0,M,2M,...,(r-1)M}.
```

For `a<b` in `D_r`, write

```text
d=b-a,  u=a^2/d,  v=b^2/d,  h=ab/d.
```

These are integers because, for `a=iM,b=jM`, the integer `j-i` divides
`M`.  Let `C_r` be the maximum of the finitely many endpoint bounds

```text
v,  u+v-b.
```

For sufficiently large `t` with `t=1 mod M` (in particular, larger than all
the finitely many kernel coefficients), define

```text
B=t^2,
R_a={ [i(t+a)]_B : -C_r<=i<t+C_r }.
```

Then `|R_a|=t+O_r(1)`.  The homomorphism

```text
phi(i,j)=i(t+a)+j(t+b) mod B
```

has kernel basis

```text
p=(t-h,u),       q=(-v,t-b+v),
```

with

```text
phi(p)=phi(q)=B,     det(p,q)=B.
```

Here the equalities for `phi` mean integer multiples `B`, hence zero modulo
`B`.  Since `t=1 mod M`, at least one of `t+a,t+b` is a unit modulo `t^2`
(when `a=0`, use `t+b`), so `phi` is onto and its kernel has index `B`.
The sublattice generated by `p,q` also has index `|det(p,q)|=B`; it is
therefore the full kernel.  Its fundamental parallelogram lies inside the
displayed index box by the
definition of `C_r`.  Hence

```text
R_a+R_b mod B = Z/BZ
```

for every distinct `a,b`.  This proves a `K_r` of modular pair states with
asymptotically unit tile cost.  The symbolic construction is audited for
`r=2,...,8`; the specialized, much smaller seven-slope set

```text
{0,10,12,15,20,30,60}
```

has phase radius 120 and is checked both symbolically and literally.

For a pair state `e={a,b}`, split its integer sum footprint into a lower mask
`L_e` and a carry mask `U_e`.  Modular coverage says exactly

```text
L_e union U_e=[0,B-1].
```

Thus `e->e` is a legal transition.  A macro block is certified when the same
role-pair sumset contains both adjacent macro sums.  With one small direct
bootstrap tile, every finite certificate of role cost `L` covering `m`
macro squares proves

```text
alpha_- >= m/L^2.
```

This is an exact, scalable point-level amplifier theorem, but only for its
stated temporal predicate.

### The cross-state obstruction

A transition `e->f` requires

```text
U_e union L_f=[0,B-1].
```

The modular-cover lemma proves this only when `e=f`.  At `t=251`, the exact
seven-slope transition graph has 25 edges: 21 self-loops and four cross-state
edges.  Independent bitset diagnostics show that the abundance of transitions
at small `t` is caused by the fixed phase padding, not by the asymptotic
tiles: the counts at `t=101,151,251` are respectively `376,254,25` out of
441, while the largest transition-hole sets are `239,2134,8175` points.

Consequently neither `K7` nor the unbounded `K_r` family can simply color the
coordinates of an arbitrary basis.  A successful use needs one of:

- a temporal role-assignment theorem producing long runs of one pair state;
- a richer automaton with near-universal cross-state transitions; or
- a low-additive-rank description of all cross-state holes, allowing an
  `o(k)` absorber.

None is proved here.

## 2. Approximate raw tiles: a rigorous no-go and a carry escape

The analytic lane proves that three `q+o(q)` integer tiles cannot have all
three raw pair-sums cover `q^2-o(q^2)` points of length-`q^2` intervals.
Pair counting first forces each normalized convolution to be close in total
variation to a translated uniform interval law.  Compactness then gives

```text
mu_1*mu_2=mu_1*mu_3=mu_2*mu_3=Unif[-1/2,1/2].
```

The characteristic functions must coincide and square to a sinc function,
which is impossible because the sinc function has simple nonzero zeros while
the zeros of an entire square have even multiplicity.  Thus asymptotically
efficient **one-raw-pair-per-block** interaction graphs remain triangle-free.

Carry really does evade that theorem.  For `q>=3`, set

```text
B=q^2-q+1,
X=(q-1)[0,q-1],
Y=[0,q-1],
Z={0} union {1+qj:0<=j<=q-2}.
```

Then

```text
X+Y=Y+Z=[0,B-1],
X+Z mod B=Z/BZ.
```

The third raw sum misses a positive fraction of every translated `B`-window,
so it does not contradict the analytic theorem.  Its lower and carry masks
are complementary enough that consecutive `XZ` macro sums cover a full
block.  This gives an asymptotically no-slack three-role carry triangle.
Its transition automaton has a one-way trap, however: for `q>=4`, direct
states cannot enter the split `XZ` state.  It therefore does not supply the
missing general temporal theorem.

## 3. Rank-one absorption and the exact remaining analytic target

If the holes of a preliminary basis lie in `U+V`, adjoining `U union V`
repairs all of them at cost `|U|+|V|`.  In particular, holes with the same
residue pattern `R` across block starts `S` cost only `|S|+|R|`, even when
every block is bad.

This yields an exact conditional closure theorem: if constructions at the
`alpha_+` endpoint leave holes contained in an additive rectangle of total
side cost `o(k)`, then `alpha_-=alpha_+`.

The missing lemma is therefore sharper than “few holes.”  It is enough to
organize all carry-transition residuals into one or finitely many additive
rectangles of sublinear total side cost.  Independent rounding cannot do
this, and the present seven-slope transition holes are not shown to have it.

## 4. Proof-producing finite search

The search lane found a separate exact finite seven-chromatic object.  In a
`3^2` block, fourteen four-point types have a 59-edge direct-complete
compatibility graph with

```text
clique number 6,  chromatic number 7.
```

The graph is vertex-critical.  A six-coloring CNF with 84 variables and 578
clauses is UNSAT; CaDiCaL's DRAT proof was independently verified by
`drat-trim` and is retained compressed with hashes.  Positive seven-colorings,
all vertex-deletion six-colorings, edges, and nonedge witnesses are rechecked.

This proves that seven-chromatic behavior is not confined to a displayed
clique.  Its tile cost is `4/3`, so it is not an asymptotically lossless
family.  Exact affine-line and small union-state searches found no nontrivial
carry SCC in their stated scopes.  These are finite negative diagnostics, not
asymptotic impossibility theorems.

## 5. What would now constitute a full solution

The most concrete route is a **temporal representation theorem**.  One needs
to show that bases approaching `alpha_+` can be assigned to an efficient
carry language with `k+o(k)` total role cost and with every target certified
through legal consecutive states.  The unbounded `K_r` construction removes
all fixed chromatic barriers, but not this temporal condition.

The analytic alternative is the **carry-rectangle lemma**: construct a
near-endpoint preliminary basis whose uncovered points lie in `U+V` with
`|U|+|V|=o(k)`.  The rank-one absorber would then finish the proof
immediately.

The attack therefore advances the frontier from “find seven efficient tile
types” to a precise global question about transitions between their
point-footprint states.  It does not establish equality of the liminf and
limsup.
