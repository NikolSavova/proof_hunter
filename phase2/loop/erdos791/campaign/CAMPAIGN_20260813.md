# Erdős #791: significant resolution campaign (2026-08-13)

## Bottom line

This campaign did **not** resolve Erdős #791 and did not improve Jukka
Kohonen's construction constant

```text
85/294 = 0.2891156462...
```

for `liminf n(k)/k^2` (equivalently `294/85` on the construction side for
`g(N)^2/N`).  It produced three rigorous advances:

1. a complete optimization theorem for the natural four-parameter block
   family underlying Kohonen's construction, proving that `85/294` is its
   unique maximum;
2. proof-certified local nonexistence results around both the 42-segment
   Kohonen placement and a smaller 20-segment, one-square-short extremizer;
3. a new-to-this-campaign fourth elementary segment with an exact phased
   tiling lemma, enlarging the finite construction language from three
   geometric directions to four.

The search and audit artifacts are sufficient to reproduce every positive
identity and to distinguish theorem, proof-certified finite result, bounded
solver result, and heuristic failure.

## 1. Structural theorem: Kohonen's whole block family is exhausted

For integers `h>=1`, `b>=h+1`, `n>=h`, and `r>=0`, put

```text
u = (h+1)n+2h
B = 2u+h(b-h-1)
D = u+hb+1

I = {0,h} union (u+h[0,b-1])
J = 2h+(h+1)[0,n-1]
K = [0,h-1] union Union_{v=0}^{r-1}[B+vD,B+vD+h].
```

These lists certify the generalized-Mrose square prefix of length

```text
ell = n+b+h+2+r(h+1)
m   = 2u+h(b-h-1)+r(u+hb+1).
```

The coverage proof is in `theory/THEORY_NOTES.md`; its only nontrivial local
ingredient is a bounded `h,h+1` coin lemma, for which the finite coefficient
bounds are written explicitly.

**Theorem.** Every member of this family satisfies

```text
m/ell^2 <= 85/294,
```

with equality only at `(h,b,n,r)=(5,6,17,2)`, Kohonen's placement.

For fixed `ell,h,r`, moving one unit from `b` to `n` increases `m`, so an
optimum has `b=h+1`.  With `a=h+1`, `s=r+2`, and `L=ell`, the optimization
reduces to

```text
m = saL-(s^2-s+2)a^2+2a-s-2,   L>=(s+1)a.
```

A continuous bound disposes of all but five finite rows; exact rational checks
on those rows give maxima `32/121`, `15/52`, `85/294`, `553/1936`, and
`3/11`, with unique equality at `(s,a,L)=(4,6,42)`.  Two independent scripts
reproduce the algebraic check and exhaust 270,114 valid parameter tuples
through `ell=100`.

Consequences:

- changing only Kohonen's module lengths cannot improve the record;
- the obvious mixed-radix composition with an ordinary finite 2-basis loses a
  factor of at least `1/4` in density and cannot improve the record;
- an exact enumeration of 9,453,780 coherent AP/interval layouts around the
  42-segment architecture found Kohonen as the unique best encountered layout
  and no `m=511` certificate.

The last item is a finite enumeration, not a theorem about arbitrary lists.

## 2. Exact local barriers, with checked UNSAT proofs

The proof-producing encoding uses the exact sufficient predicate

```text
q in I+J  or  q in I+K  or  {q-1,q} subset J+K.
```

It was validated in the positive direction by decoding the known seeds and
checking literal expanded bases at `t=2,3,5,10` with the independent parent
verifier.  All negative instances were solved by CaDiCaL and their DRAT traces
were independently accepted by `drat-trim`.

### Kohonen target

For `ell=42`, counts `(8,17,17)`, coordinate domain `[0,510]`, and target
`m=511`, there is no solution within one, two, or three coordinate replacements
of the consistently J/K-normalized Kohonen seed.  Radius three required a CNF
with 418,329 variables and 1,661,991 clauses; its checker performed
108,746,580 resolution steps.

### Smaller target

The block-family member `(h,b,n,r)=(3,4,7,1)` gives

```text
ell=20, m=115, counts (6,7,7),
I=[0,3,34,37,40,43],
J=[6,10,14,18,22,26,30],
K=[0,1,2,68,69,70,71].
```

Since `85*20^2/294=115.64...`, an `m=116` certificate would improve the
asymptotic record.  After consistent J/K normalization, exact SAT excludes
replacement radii one through four.  The radius-four trace was independently
verified using 35,576,353 resolution steps.

These are local results for fixed type counts and the stated sufficient
predicate.  They do not exclude radius four or larger at `ell=42`, radius five
or larger at `ell=20`, different count splits, different finite predicates,
or a solution by another construction.

### Important correction

The preliminary CP-SAT scripts normalized `0 in J` but measured distance from
the unswapped published seeds, where `0 in K`.  Their old radius claims were
therefore invalid.  Those artifacts are marked superseded.  The results above
come from a replacement encoding that swaps the seed labels consistently,
passes radius-zero positive controls, and has independently checked DRAT
proofs.  This correction was found adversarially, not hidden.

## 3. Fourth direction: a phased reflected-diagonal lemma

For even `t`, write `Q=[0,t^2-1]` and define the four elementary segments

```text
V=[0,t]
H={it:0<=i<t}
S={i(t+1):0<=i<t}
T={j(t-1):0<=j<=t}.
```

The new exact identities are

```text
V+T contains Q,
(H+T) union (H+T+t^2) contains Q+t^2,
```

and, writing `E=S+T`, both

```text
E union (E+t^2+1)
(E+1) union (E+t^2)
```

contain `Q+t^2`.  The final pair is the key: copies of `T` with bounded phases
zero and one make the reflected/slanted sum tile a complete square.

For macro lists `I,J,K,L0,L1`, this gives the sufficient predicate

```text
q in I+J, I+K, or I+L0;
{q-1,q} subset J+K;
{q-1,q} subset J+L0;
q-1 in K+L0 and q in K+L1; or
q-1 in K+L1 and q in K+L0.
```

A certified prefix of length `m`, using
`ell=|I|+|J|+|K|+|L0|+|L1|`, yields the asymptotic constant `m/ell^2`.
Four geometric types have a pair-counting ceiling `3/8`, rather than the
three-direction ceiling `1/3`; this is only a ceiling, not an achieved ratio.

The phase is essential.  Without it, two consecutive `S+T` translates miss
exactly

```text
floor((t-1)^2/4)
```

points of the next square.  The identities were checked through `t=100`, the
hole formula through `t=500`, and 12,994 randomly certified macro squares by
literal expansion.  A targeted literature search found Kohonen's existing
three-direction paper but no direct antecedent for this exact phased lemma;
the safe claim is **new to this campaign**, not literature-new.

No record placement was found in this language.  Exact all-type-split CP-SAT
sweeps excluded `ell=7,m=15` and `ell=8,m=19` inside the finite predicate;
larger record targets at `ell=18,20,24` timed out without a conclusion.

## 4. Search actually performed

The cheap baseline evaluated 332,498 candidates around Kohonen.  The
incremental C++ search then used more than 300 million proposals across seeded,
guided-hole, enlarged-`ell`, and random-start runs.  In particular:

| target | fixed counts | proposals | best certified |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `m=511` | `(8,17,17)` | 100,000,000 | 510/511 |
| `m=511`, guided repair | `(8,17,17)` | 50,000,000 | 510/511 |
| `m=535` | `(9,17,17)` | 50,000,000 | 518/535 |
| `m=116`, guided repair | `(6,7,7)` | 100,000,000 | 115/116 |

Five-million-proposal random starts at every `ell=18,...,30` also missed the
record thresholds.  These are heuristic failures, not nonexistence evidence.

At the 20-segment seed, moving `68` to `72` in the last block fills the new
boundary square but transports the unique hole to 68.  Exhausting the entire
single-coordinate connected component while allowing up to six temporary
holes visits 4,002 states and finds no certificate.  This explains why simple
hole repair stalls, while leaving nonlocal changes fully open.

Global exact searches at `(42,511)` and `(18,94)` exhausted their time budgets
and are recorded as `UNKNOWN`, never as UNSAT.  A new unrestricted
proof-producing run at `(20,116)`, counts `(6,7,7)`, was likewise stopped
`UNKNOWN` after 1,431,127 conflicts; its partial DRAT trace is not a proof and
was discarded.

## 6. Best next attack

The highest-value unresolved finite question is global feasibility of the
three-tile `(ell,m)=(20,116)`, `(6,7,7)` instance.  It is small enough for a
serious proof-producing SAT portfolio, and any SAT witness is immediately a
strict asymptotic improvement.  The current radius-four closure says that a
witness, if one exists, requires a genuinely coordinated nonlocal repair.

In parallel mathematically, the new fourth-direction language should be
searched over arithmetic blocks with forced alternation between `L0` and
`L1`, not by generic coordinate annealing.  A chain that makes `K+L0` and
`K+L1` alternate on long intervals is the most concrete route by which the
phase lemma could turn its higher counting ceiling into an actual record.

Until one of those routes produces a verified certificate, the honest status
is: **substantial structural and proof-certified progress, but Erdős #791
remains open.**
